# Privacy Policy

## Local-First Architecture

Fragment is built with a **local-first** architecture. Your data lives on your device, not on our servers.

### Data Storage

- **Primary Storage**: SQLite database on your device (via IndexedDB/OPFS in browser)
- **No Cloud Storage**: By default, no data is sent to our servers
- **User Control**: You own and control all your data

### What We Don't Collect

Fragment does not collect:

- Personal information
- Usage analytics
- Behavior tracking
- Device identifiers
- Location data
- Network information

### Optional Sync (Future Feature)

If we add optional cloud sync in the future:

- Sync will be **opt-in only**
- Data will be **encrypted end-to-end**
- You can **disable sync at any time**
- You can **delete all synced data**

We will never enable sync without your explicit consent.

## AI Processing

When using AI features:

- Prompts are sent to AI providers (OpenAI/Anthropic) via server-side proxy
- **No conversation history** is stored by AI providers
- **No user data** is used to train AI models
- AI responses are **stateless** and **ephemeral**

We use AI providers that commit to:

- Not using your data for training
- Not storing conversation history
- Privacy-first data handling

## Third-Party Services

Fragment may use:

- **AI Providers** (OpenAI/Anthropic): For pattern surfacing only, stateless
- **Payment Processors** (Stripe): For premium features, payment data only
- **Optional Sync** (Supabase/Cloudflare D1): If enabled, encrypted end-to-end

We do not share your fragment data with any third parties except:

- AI providers (for processing only, not storage)
- Payment processors (for payment processing only)

## Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time:

- **Local Data**: Delete the app or clear browser storage
- **Synced Data**: If sync is enabled, delete from sync provider
- **Account Data**: If accounts are added, delete account to remove all data

## Children's Privacy

Fragment is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes to Privacy Policy

We will notify users of any material changes to this privacy policy.
